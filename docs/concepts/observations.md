# Observations, evidence, and responses

An observation declares what must be learned; a resource performs it; evidence records how we know; a structured finding interprets the evidence; observed state can then reveal a gap or drift; policy selects a response. Initial well-known observation types are metric, event, semantic, assertion, and external-state, while extension identifiers remain valid.

Semantic Alerts are a product presentation. A semantic observation is evaluated into a semantic finding. An alert may be routed from that finding, but the same finding can update health, create work, notify an actor, produce drift, or generate a plan.
