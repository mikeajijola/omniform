# From founding draft to Omniform

A founding session is a review workflow, not a Company definition. It may contain proposed, accepted, rejected, and edited items plus assumptions, questions, confidence, rationale, risks, priorities, and alternative designs. Those workflow fields are non-canonical and MUST NOT appear in portable Omniform.

Commit follows a strict boundary:

1. Select only accepted canonical company, outcome, capability, policy, observation, and resource-requirement items.
2. Convert them into an Omniform definition.
3. Validate the definition against Omniform.
4. Persist that desired definition separately from OmniSeed state, evidence, and runtime metadata.
5. Let OmniSeed calculate gaps and plans honestly.

Capabilities precede resources. A required capability without a realisation is valid and begins as `missing`; founding MUST NOT fabricate agents or deployed resources. Assumptions, open questions, rationale, confidence, and alternative designs remain in the founding record or advisory material.
