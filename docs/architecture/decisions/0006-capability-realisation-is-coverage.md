# 0006 — Capability realisation is evidence-backed coverage

Status: accepted

## Decision

Company capabilities declare required lower-level abilities. Resources and reusable assets expose capability offerings. A selected CapabilityRealisation composes agents, skills, connectors, workflows, schedules, systems, people, partners, and machines, while deployment state and evidence determine whether that composition actually operates.

Omniform remains strategy-neutral. Agent-first, human-first, privacy-first, cost, locality, and risk preferences are policy/strategy inputs rather than ontology rules. Schedule is a portable invocation-time primitive and remains separate from Workflow and provider implementation.

## Consequences

A resource attachment alone cannot establish realised state. Human dependency is explicit and qualified. Missing, partial, blocked, deferred, accepted-gap, retryable, realised, and retired outcomes can be represented without invalidating desired company intent.
