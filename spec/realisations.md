# Capability requirements, offerings, and realisations

A company Capability MAY declare lower-level `requirements`. A Resource, Skill, Connector, or Workflow MAY expose actor-neutral capability `offers`. A CapabilityRealisation records the selected composition intended to fulfil the company capability; it does not claim that those resources are deployed or healthy.

Implementations MUST calculate coverage from active offerings, policy requirements, and evidence. Merely attaching one resource does not realise a capability. Missing human authority MUST be represented as a precise requirement rather than a vague dependency. Accepted or deferred gaps remain valid declared company state.

Agents, people, machines, skills, connectors, workflows, schedules, systems, partners, and providers are realisation mechanisms. None competes with Capability as the primary organisational abstraction.
